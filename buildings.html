About BSC Buildings

Building Solutions

We are a family owned business and have been since 1946. With over 70 years of experience at Boettcher and Sons. The BSC Building division started in 1974. We have nearly 40 years of metal building experience, and our friendly employees will work to fit your needs. All of our buildings are made to your specifications and engineer drawings, whether you need an outhouse or a barn or anything in between. We can deliver finished buildings, with everything you need or we can work on site.
