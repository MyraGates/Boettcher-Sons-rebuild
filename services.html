Custom metalwork

Fabrication

Our fabrication shop can build what you need, from handrails and walkways to flagpoles and sunshades. Tell us what you want, and we will help make it a reality.

You can bring us your design, or our friendly staff can help you decide what you want and how we can make it for a reasonable price.

We can build from your choice of materials, including steel, aluminum, stainless steel, and galvanized alloys. We also offer delivery and installation.

Repair and maintenance

Repair

If it's broken, we can fix it. Our goal is to fix it right and solve the problem rather than create more problems.

We repair many things, including pipes, trailers, buckets, and equipment.

If you can't bring it to us, our welding trucks can come to you and make repairs on site. Walk-ins are welcome, and we can often repair your item on the spot or have it back to you the same day.
